NewsHome Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

NewsHome is a Safari extension that shows news, weather and market data on your new tab page. It does not collect, store or share any personal data with its developer. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings (name for the greeting, city, sections, theme) and any holdings you import are saved in Safari's local storage on your Mac. They are never sent to us.

Requests to content providers

To show content, NewsHome requests public data directly from the providers:

  • News headlines from the publishers' public RSS feeds (for example The New York Times, BBC, Google News).
  • Weather from Open-Meteo, which receives the name or coordinates of the city you set.
  • Stock quotes from Yahoo Finance, which receives the ticker symbols to price. Share counts, costs and values are never sent.

These providers receive the standard information any web request includes, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Clicking a story opens the publisher's website.
