NewsHome

An ad-free new tab page for Safari on the Mac: top stories, local news and weather, markets, tech, health and recipes.

Set up

  1. Open the NewsHome app once.
  2. Safari → Settings → Extensions: tick NewsHome.
  3. Safari → Settings → General: set New tabs open with to NewsHome.
  4. To use it on your other Macs, tick Share across devices in Safari's Extensions settings.
